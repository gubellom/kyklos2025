This folder contains the three waves from the European Social Survey: "ESS3.dta," "ESS6.dta," and "ESS7.dta." The do.file "merging.do" appends the three waves to a unique file generating the final dataset "ESS367.dta," which also appears in the folder "datasets.

References for data can be found at:

ESS3.dta: European Social Survey (ESS 3). 2018. European Social Survey European Research Infrastructure (ESS ERIC), ESS3—Integrated File, Edition 3.7 (Latvia and Romania Not Included). Sikt—Norwegian Agency for Shared Services in Education and Research. https://doi.org/10.21338/ESS3E03_7. 

ESS6.dta: European Social Survey (ESS 6). 2023. European Social Survey European Research Infrastructure (ESS Eric), ESS6—Integrated File, Edition 2.6. Sikt—Norwegian Agency for Shared Services in Education and Research. https://doi.org/10.21338/ess6e02_6. 

ESS7.dta: European Social Survey (ESS 7). 2018. European Social Survey European Research Infrastructure (ESS ERIC), ESS7—Integrated File, Edition 2.3. Sikt—Norwegian Agency for Shared Services in Education and Research. https://doi.org/10.21338/ess7e02_3.
